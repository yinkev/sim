## Final synthesis

### Finalized architecture correction

Use this primitive split:

```text
Actor
Profile
Producer / Capability
Raw Event
Observation
Evidence
Loop
Decision
Feature Projection
Prediction Summary
Recommendation
Action Proposal
Outcome
Policy
```

### Definitions

- `Actor`: who performed or asserted something: human, scheduler, workflow, agent, integration, system, prediction model.
- `Profile`: isolated local identity/data container for one dogfood user.
- `Producer / Capability`: a module that emits raw events/observations or provides actions.
- `Raw Event`: append-only fact that happened.
- `Observation`: semantic interpretation derived from raw event(s).
- `Evidence`: artifact, source, proof, output, test, diff, log, receipt.
- `Loop`: ongoing domain of work/study/life requiring state, action, and review.
- `Decision`: accepted choice with rationale, actor, and evidence.
- `Feature Projection`: deterministic derived signal from events/observations.
- `Prediction Summary`: probabilistic estimate with confidence, data sufficiency, drivers, and model version.
- `Recommendation`: suggested next move based on observations/predictions/policy.
- `Action Proposal`: reviewable change before execution.
- `Outcome`: observed result after action/recommendation.
- `Policy`: local privacy/read/write/export/delete rules.
